Why did I get these results?

Each scheme has published eligibility rules (age, income, occupation, category, and similar). We compared your five answers against those rules. "Eligible" means you meet every rule we checked; "Partial match" means you meet most of them but one detail (like an exact income cut-off in your state) needs checking on the official portal.

This is a hackathon prototype built to demonstrate a better citizen journey. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to the Government of India. Always confirm details on the official scheme portal before applying.
