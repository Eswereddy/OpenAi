Am I Eligible?

India runs 1,000+ welfare schemes. Most eligible citizens never find out they qualify. This app collapses discovery into one 2-minute conversation — in English, Hindi, or Telugu — and never invents an answer: every AI layer explains a verdict a deterministic rule engine already computed.

Why this is different from a typical hackathon eligibility-checker demo

Rule engine decides, AI only explains

Every eligibility verdict comes from a deterministic, testable rule engine. AI never decides who qualifies — it only turns an already-computed verdict into plain language, an action plan, or an answer to a follow-up question.

Never goes down

Three-provider AI failover (Groq → OpenAI → Anthropic) means one outage doesn't break the demo — and with zero keys configured, every feature still works via a deterministic template, so a bad wifi day at judging never costs you a feature.

Built for the real user, not the demo

Offline-capable rule engine, voice input/output, high-contrast mode, adjustable text size, WhatsApp share, and calendar reminders — for a citizen on a low-end phone and a bad connection, not just for a judge on a laptop.

Tested, not just demoed

83+ automated checks across unit tests (pure rule/validation logic) and a zero-dependency smoke suite that boots the real server and exercises every API route — npm test, no mocking framework required.

Genuinely agentic, not a chatbot skin

The AI Investigator runs a real multi-step tool-calling loop (up to 4 steps) over 4 read-only tools, and returns its own reasoning trace — not one fixed prompt with a fixed answer shape.

Provenance over confidence

Every scheme shows its source, last-verified date, and a version number. A citizen thumbs-down on a match feeds a real feedback table used to flag which scheme's rules need a human re-check first.

How the pieces connect (one journey, not five bolt-ons)

Speak/type one sentence or tap a 1-click example
AI extracts structured fields → form pre-fills → citizen reviews/edits
Live on-device match count + ₹ estimate while the form is completed
Rule engine decides eligibility — never AI
AI summary explains the verdict in plain language → 🔊 read aloud
🔧 Income slider re-runs the same rules instantly, no AI call
🧭 AI action plan + 📋 AI document checklist, both readable aloud
🔎 AI Investigator answers open-ended follow-ups with a visible reasoning trace
🔔 Calendar reminder / 🖨 Print / 📲 WhatsApp share to hand off to family
